# Coastal PPO — Out-of-Network Policy (Synthetic)

**Tenant:** `coastal-ppo`  
**Document ID:** `oon-policy`

---

## Balance billing

Out-of-network providers may bill members for amounts not paid by the plan. The plan does not protect members from balance billing for OON services.

**Section:** `oon.balance-billing`

---

## Laparoscopic procedures

Cholecystectomy (47562) covered OON subject to allowed amount calculation. Assistant surgeon lines may be separately reviewed; unbundling and assistant surgeon denials require specific coding review.

**Section:** `surgical.cholecystectomy`

---

## Provider resubmission

Corrected claims must include valid NPI, correct place of service, and appropriate modifiers. Missing rendering provider identifier → RARC N290.

**Section:** `billing.resubmission`
