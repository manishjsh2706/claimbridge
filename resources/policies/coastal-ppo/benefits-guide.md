## Network tiers

Coastal PPO offers **in-network** and **out-of-network** benefits. Out-of-network providers may balance bill members for amounts above allowed.

**Section:** `network.tiers`

---

## Out-of-network surgical benefits

Out-of-network facility and professional claims pay at **60% of allowed amount** after deductible. Member responsible for:

- Deductible (if not met)
- Coinsurance on allowed amount
- **Difference between billed and allowed** (balance billing permitted OON)

**Section:** `oon.surgical`

Adjustment CO-45 reflects charge above allowed; member EOB must explain OON patient responsibility clearly.

---

## Cosmetic and elective exclusions

Services primarily for cosmetic improvement are **not covered**, including elective dermatology procedures unless medically necessary for documented disease.

**Section:** `exclusions.cosmetic`

- CPT 11900 and similar cosmetic dermatology → denial CO-50 when cosmetic exclusion applies

---

## Appeals

Commercial PPO members: **30 days** from determination letter. 1-800-555-0198.

**Section:** `appeals.member`
