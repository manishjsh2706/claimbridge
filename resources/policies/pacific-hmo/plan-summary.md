# Pacific HMO — Plan Summary (Synthetic)

**Tenant:** `pacific-hmo`  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Document ID:** `plan-summary`

---

## Overview

Pacific HMO is a regional HMO with a **narrow network**. Members must use in-network PCPs for most services unless authorized.

---

## Fee schedule and allowed amounts

### Out-of-pocket on allowed amounts

When a provider bills above the plan **allowed amount**, the plan pays based on the allowed amount only. The member is not responsible for the difference between billed and allowed for **in-network** services (balance billing prohibited for in-network).

**Section:** `fee-schedule.allowed-amounts`

- Allowed amounts are published in the Pacific HMO provider fee schedule
- Member copay/coinsurance applies to **allowed amount**, not billed charge
- Adjustment code CO-45 commonly applies when billed exceeds allowed

---

## Prior authorization

### Imaging and advanced imaging

MRI, CT, and PET scans require **prior authorization** except when performed in emergency department for acute symptoms documented in claim.

**Section:** `prior-auth.imaging`

- Lumbar spine MRI (72148) requires prior auth for non-emergency settings
- Failure to obtain prior auth may result in denial (CO-197)

---

## Preventive care

Annual wellness visits (99395) covered 100% when in-network and after deductible met per plan tier.

**Section:** `preventive.wellness`

---

## Member appeals

Members may appeal adverse determinations within **60 days** of EOB date. Contact member services at 1-800-555-0142.

**Section:** `appeals.member`
