ClaimBridge - VectorDB Retrieval: How Tenant Isolation Works

🔍 Comparison: WITH vs WITHOUT Tenant Filter

❌ WITHOUT Tenant Filter

VectorDB Query:
.with_near_vector(embedding)
.with_limit(5) ← Only top-5, NO filter!

Problem:
Top-5 most similar policies from ALL tenants combined!

Result:
If Coastal has similar policy language, Coastal policy appears in Pacific query!

❌ CROSS-TENANT LEAKAGE!

✅ WITH Tenant Filter (CORRECT)

VectorDB Query:
.with_near_vector(embedding)
.with_where({"tenant_id": "pacific-hmo"})
.with_limit(5)

Solution:
Top-5 most similar ONLY from Pacific policies!

Result:
GUARANTEED: No Coastal leakage. 100% isolation.

✅ ISOLATION ENFORCED!

🎯 Why This Matters for Your Interview

"I designed the RAG layer to filter VectorDB retrieval by tenant_id at query time. This ensures that when a Pacific member submits a claim, their LLM context ONLY contains Pacific policies. We embed the tenant filter directly into the Weaviate query, not as a post-processing step. This prevents accidental cross-tenant leakage at the source — the most secure approach."
